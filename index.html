Privacy Architecture

Your data isn't a product.
It's a possession.

ControlFreak is built on a two-tier privacy model. Personal data stays local. Shared data stays isolated. No exceptions, no opt-ins that quietly become opt-outs.

Local-Only Storage

Wellness, sleep, workout, and personal data is stored in an on-device SQLite database. It is never transmitted to any server — not even ours.

No Behavioural Profiling

We collect no analytics on how you use the app. No telemetry, no crash reports tied to your identity, no ad targeting — ever.

HealthKit Respect

Health data read via Apple HealthKit is processed on-device only. We follow Apple's HealthKit data usage guidelines strictly.

Minimal Cloud Footprint

Only Group Plan features use cloud infrastructure — and only the data necessary to coordinate with other users. Your identity remains pseudonymous.

How It Works

Calm through competence.

  1. Connect Apple Health

    Grant read access to HealthKit. ControlFreak pulls your workouts, sleep stages, and activity data — all processed locally on your device.

  2. Set Up Your Dashboard

    Choose which wellness tiles matter to you. Drag to reorder, hide what you don't need, and create custom habit tiles for your personal rituals.

  3. Organise Your Work

    Add your schedule, tasks, and focus blocks to the Work view. Everything stays on-device and integrates with your existing calendar.

  4. Coordinate Without Compromise

    Create Group Plans for outings, dinners, or trips. Share logistics with specific people without exposing your personal wellness or work data.

About ControlFreak

Built for personal sovereignty.

ControlFreak was born out of frustration with productivity apps that either do too little or invade your privacy to do it. Most apps fragment your life across six tools, sell your habits to advertisers, and bury useful data in dashboards designed to maximise engagement — not clarity.

We believe your data belongs to you, your patterns are yours alone, and software should earn your attention by being genuinely useful — not by manufacturing dependency.

ControlFreak is designed for people who want to see their life clearly, act on it deliberately, and move through the day without friction. It is opinionated about privacy, minimal about noise, and precise about the details that actually matter.

Minimalist Precision

Every feature earns its place. Nothing is added because a competitor has it.

Calm Through Competence

The app should reduce cognitive load, not add to it. Information density without visual noise.

Privacy as a Constraint

Privacy is not a feature toggle — it is a design constraint that shapes every architectural decision.

iOS Native First

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 May 2026

Overview

ControlFreak ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and how it is protected. By using ControlFreak, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Data We Access

ControlFreak may request access to the following on your device:

  • Apple HealthKit — workout sessions, sleep data, activity rings, and related health metrics. This data is read-only and processed entirely on your device.
  • Calendar — to display your schedule in the Work view. We do not modify your calendar without explicit action from you.
  • Location — optionally, for logistics and group plan features. Location data is not stored on our servers.

Data Storage

All personal, wellness, and work data is stored locally on your device using an on-device SQLite database. This data is never transmitted to our servers.

Group Plan data — limited to itinerary stops, timing, and group membership — may be stored in our cloud infrastructure (Supabase) solely to enable coordination with other users you have explicitly invited. This data does not include your health, sleep, workout, or personal notes.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect analytics on your app usage.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising purposes.
  • We do not build behavioural profiles.
  • We do not use your HealthKit data for advertising or user profiling.

Third-Party Services

ControlFreak uses the following third-party services in limited contexts:

  • Mapbox — for map rendering in logistics features. Mapbox may receive anonymised map tile requests.
  • Supabase — for Group Plan cloud storage. Supabase is hosted in the European Union and is subject to GDPR-compliant data handling.
  • Foursquare / Venue APIs — for place search in logistics features. Only the search query is sent; no personal data is attached.

HealthKit Data

ControlFreak accesses Apple HealthKit data in accordance with Apple's HealthKit policies. HealthKit data is used solely to display your health metrics within the app. It is not shared with third parties, used for advertising, or uploaded to any server.

Children's Privacy

ControlFreak is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your Rights

Because your personal data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it at all times. You can delete the app — and all associated local data — at any time. For Group Plan data stored in the cloud, you may request deletion by contacting us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
